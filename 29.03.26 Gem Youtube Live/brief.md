# GEMS Webinar — Event Brief

## The Event
- **What:** YouTube Live webinar — Gemini Gems in Education (וובינר Gemini Gems בחינוך)
- **Date:** Sunday, March 29, 2026, 20:00–21:00 Israel time
- **Format:** Free, live on YouTube → recording sent to all registrants after
- **Audience:** Israeli teachers — beginners who want to use AI in the classroom

## Core Message (WHY → HOW → WHAT)

**WHY (the belief):**
> "המורה שמייצר כלי AI לתלמידים — הוא המורה שהם לא ישכחו"
> *(The teacher who builds AI tools for students — is the teacher they won't forget)*

**HOW (the mechanism — chef analogy):**
Regular Gemini doesn't remember you between sessions — every time from scratch.
A Gem is like your personal chef: already knows your style, your class, your students.
One sentence instead of a 10-line prompt.

**WHAT (the outcome):**
Walk away with a working Gem built live, ready to share with students the next day.

## The Hero Story — Yuval & Synapse 3

The emotional core of the entire campaign:

- Omri built **Synapse 3** (סינאפסה 3) — a Gem for his biology students
- A student named **Yuval**, who struggles and doesn't always show up to class, sent a message at **2am** — critiquing the answer the Gem gave him
- This proves what a good Gem does: it brings the student back, even when you're not there
