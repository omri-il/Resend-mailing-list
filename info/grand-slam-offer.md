# Grand Slam Offer Structure

How to create offers so good people feel stupid saying no.

**Hormozi's Definition:** A Grand Slam Offer solves every barrier preventing the customer from achieving their dream outcome, delivered in a way that removes friction, at a price that feels like a steal.

---

## The 5-Step Framework

### Step 1: Define Dream Outcome

**NOT the product. NOT the method. The END RESULT.**

**Discovery Questions (ask during brainstorming):**

1. What's the ultimate goal? (what do they REALLY want?)
2. How will they know they've succeeded? (specific, measurable)
3. What emotional benefit comes from achieving this? (status, relief, pride, freedom)

**Example:**

❌ Bad (product-focused):
> "I want to teach them digital marketing"

✅ Good (outcome-focused):
> "They want a steady stream of qualified leads without depending on expensive ads or cold outreach"

---

### Step 2: List ALL Barriers

**Why haven't they achieved the dream outcome yet?**

**Common Barrier Categories:**

| Category | Example Barriers |
|----------|------------------|
| **Knowledge** | "I don't know what to post", "I don't understand SEO" |
| **Skills** | "I can't write good copy", "I'm not tech-savvy" |
| **Time** | "I work full-time", "I have no time to learn" |
| **Money** | "Ads are expensive", "Already spent $ on courses that didn't work" |
| **Fear** | "What if I fail again?", "What if I look stupid?" |
| **Tools** | "I don't have the right software", "My website is outdated" |
| **Support** | "I have no one to ask for help", "I'm doing this alone" |

**Barrier Identification Exercise:**

Ask the client or research:

1. What have you tried before?
2. Why didn't it work?
3. What's stopping you from starting now?
4. What are you afraid might happen?
5. What resources do you feel you lack?

**Goal:** Identify 5-10 barriers minimum.

---

### Step 3: Create Solutions (1:1 Mapping)

**For each barrier, create a specific solution.**

**Template:**

```
Barrier: [What stops them]
Solution: [Your specific answer to this]
Value: [Why this matters / what this enables]
```

**Example Mapping:**

| # | Barrier | Solution | Value |
|---|---------|----------|-------|
| 1 | "I don't know what content to post" | 50 done-for-you post templates | Post daily without creative burnout |
| 2 | "I tried ads and lost money" | Risk-free 60-day guarantee | Try without financial risk |
| 3 | "I don't have time to learn complex tools" | 3-step system (15 min/day) | Implement while working full-time |
| 4 | "I'm afraid I'll fail again" | Weekly 1-on-1 coaching calls | Get unstuck immediately when blocked |
| 5 | "I can't afford expensive software" | All tools included (worth $400/mo) | Start with zero additional costs |

**CRITICAL:** Don't just list features. Map each feature to the specific barrier it solves.

---

### Step 4: Design Delivery Mechanism

**How is each solution delivered?**

**Delivery Formats:**

| Format | When to Use | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Done-for-you** | High-skill barrier | "We write the first 10 posts for you" |
| **Done-with-you** | Support/accountability barrier | "Weekly group coaching calls" |
| **Templates** | Time/knowledge barrier | "50 fill-in-the-blank email templates" |
| **Checklists** | Overwhelm/confusion barrier | "Daily 15-minute action plan" |
| **Software/Tools** | Resource barrier | "Lifetime access to our $99/mo tool" |
| **Community** | Isolation barrier | "Private Slack group with 200+ members" |
| **Guarantee** | Fear/risk barrier | "60-day money-back if no results" |

**Example Delivery Design:**

```
Solution: "50 done-for-you post templates"

Delivery:
- Format: PDF + Canva templates
- Access: Immediate upon enrollment
- Support: Video tutorial showing how to customize
- Update: New templates added monthly

Why this delivery works:
- No design skills needed (Canva)
- No waiting (immediate access)
- No stale content (monthly updates)
```

---

### Step 5: Enhance with Bonuses & Risk Reversal

**Bonuses = Additional barrier removers**

**Types of Bonuses:**

1. **Speed bonuses** (reduce time delay)
   - "Quick-start checklist"
   - "First 3 posts written for you"
   - "30-day fast-track roadmap"

2. **Effort bonuses** (reduce friction)
   - "Done-for-you templates"
   - "Copy-paste scripts"
   - "Plug-and-play tools"

3. **Likelihood bonuses** (increase credibility)
   - "Case study library (20+ examples)"
   - "Guarantee upgrade"
   - "Extended support period"

**Risk Reversal Options:**

| Type | Example | When to Use |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Money-back guarantee** | "60-day full refund, no questions asked" | When you're confident in results |
| **Conditional guarantee** | "Refund if you complete all modules and get zero results" | Filters out non-action-takers |
| **Performance guarantee** | "We'll work with you until you get 3 clients" | High-touch offers |
| **Free trial** | "First 14 days free, cancel anytime" | SaaS/subscription models |

**Enhancement Template:**

```markdown
## Main Offer
[Core solution with barrier mapping]

## Bonuses (Value Stacking)
1. **Bonus 1:** [Name] (Value: $X) — [How it removes a barrier]
2. **Bonus 2:** [Name] (Value: $X) — [How it removes a barrier]
3. **Bonus 3:** [Name] (Value: $X) — [How it removes a barrier]

Total Value: $[sum]
Your Investment: $[actual price]

## Guarantee
[Specific risk reversal]

## Urgency (with "Reason Why")
[Why now? Limited seats, cohort-based, seasonal relevance, etc.]
```

## Putting It Together: Full Offer Stack

### Example: Digital Marketing Course

**Dream Outcome:**
> Get 50+ qualified leads per month without expensive ads

**Barriers Identified:**
1. Don't know what content to post
2. Tried ads before, lost money
3. No time to learn complex tools
4. Afraid of another failed attempt
5. Can't afford expensive marketing software

**Grand Slam Offer:**

```markdown
## Get 50 Leads/Month in 90 Days — Without Spending $1 on Ads

### What You Get:

**Core System** ($997 value)
→ Solves Barriers 1, 3: Simple 3-step content system (15 min/day)

**Weekly 1-on-1 Coaching** ($2,400 value)
→ Solves Barrier 4: Never get stuck, immediate expert help

**Done-for-You Templates** ($500 value)
→ Solves Barrier 1: 50 pre-written posts, 20 email sequences

**Software Suite Included** ($400/mo = $4,800/year value)
→ Solves Barrier 5: All tools included, zero additional cost

### Bonuses:

1. **Quick-Start Kit** ($200 value)
   - First 10 posts written for you
   - Instant implementation

2. **Case Study Library** ($300 value)
   - 25 real businesses, what they did, exact results
   - Proof it works for people like you

3. **Private Community Access** ($1,200/year value)
   - 200+ members getting results
   - Daily support, live Q&A sessions

**Total Value:** $10,397
**Your Investment Today:** $997

### Guarantee:
If you complete all 6 modules and don't get at least 10 qualified leads in 90 days, we'll refund 100% AND give you 3 months of free coaching.

→ Solves Barrier 2 (fear of wasting money) + Barrier 4 (fear of failure)

### Why Now?
Next cohort starts March 25th. Only 20 spots available (limited by coaching capacity). Enrollment closes March 22nd.
```

## How to Present Offers on Site

### Format 1: Pricing Section (Simple)

Use for landing pages where space is limited.

**Structure:**
1. Headline: Dream outcome
2. Value stack: List solutions with values
3. Price comparison: Total value vs. actual price
4. Guarantee: Risk reversal
5. CTA: What + Next + Why

**Example:**

```json
{
  "pricing": {
    "title": "Get 50 Leads/Month — Without Expensive Ads",
    "subtitle": "Everything you need to build a lead generation system that works",
    "price": "₪997",
    "original_price": "Total Value: ₪10,397",
    "features": [
      "Core System: 3-Step Content Formula (15 min/day)",
      "Weekly 1-on-1 Coaching with Expert Marketer",
      "50 Done-for-You Post Templates + 20 Email Sequences",
      "All Software Included ($400/mo value)",
      "Bonus: Quick-Start Kit (First 10 Posts Written)",
      "Bonus: 25 Case Studies from Real Businesses",
      "Bonus: Private Community (200+ Active Members)",
      "90-Day Results Guarantee or Full Refund + Free Coaching"
    ],
    "cta_text": "Claim Your Spot → Start Getting Leads in 7 Days",
    "cta_link": "https://example.com/enroll",
    "urgency": "20 spots left | Cohort starts March 25"
  }
}
```

### Format 2: Dedicated Offer Section (Detailed)

For course pages or multi-section landing pages.

**Sections:**
1. **Hero:** Dream outcome + timeline + biggest barrier removal
2. **Problem:** Paint current pain (barriers)
3. **Solution:** How you solve each barrier
4. **Proof:** Case studies, testimonials
5. **Offer Stack:** Full value breakdown
6. **Guarantee:** Risk reversal detail
7. **FAQ:** Handle remaining objections
8. **CTA:** Final push with urgency

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| "Just listing features" | Map each feature to specific barrier it solves |
| "Vague bonuses ('Extra support')" | Be specific: "Weekly 60-min coaching calls" |
| "No value attribution" | Add dollar values to every component |
| "Weak guarantee" | Make it conditional on their effort, not results alone |
| "Fake urgency" | Always provide "reason why" (cohort-based, limited capacity, seasonal) |
| "Buried CTA" | CTA should appear 3+ times: top, middle, bottom |

---

## Barrier → Solution Checklist

Before finalizing offer, verify:

- [ ] Identified 5+ barriers (ask client/research)
- [ ] Every barrier has 1+ specific solutions
- [ ] Every solution has clear delivery mechanism
- [ ] Value assigned to each component
- [ ] Total value > 5x actual price (perceived value gap)
- [ ] Guarantee removes biggest fear
- [ ] At least 2 bonuses that reduce time or effort
- [ ] Urgency has believable "reason why"
- [ ] Offer presented in order: Problem → Solution → Proof → Stack → Guarantee
